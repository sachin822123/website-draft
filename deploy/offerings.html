A few honest questions.

Is this an agency?

No, and yes. The OS is software you operate. The Function is a weekly operator we run. Packs are large pieces of work, executed by the best operators in our bench. We don't book retainers for "marketing services" — every engagement is tied to a Market Map and a 90-day arc.

Can we start with the OS and add the Function later?

Yes. Most companies do. We set up the Market Map and Strategy in the first 2 weeks, then your team runs the weekly loop. If you decide you want us operating it, we move you to the Function tier with no setup repeat.

How are Packs priced?

Packs are scoped one-on-one. We brief the right operator from our vetted bench — directors, writers, editors, event leads — and give you a fixed price before we begin. Because we run a single bench across every customer, you get a category-leading creative at a price that's typically a fraction of an equivalent brand-name agency retainer.

Who actually does the execution?

The best operators in our bench, picked for the moment. Directors who've shipped category-defining films. Writers from category-defining publications. Event organisers behind rooms that matter. We bring them in against your plan — you don't manage them, we do.

What if our team already does some of this?

Better. The OS works with your team. The Function plugs in alongside whatever marketing or founder execution you already have — we don't replace it. We just make sure every action is tied to a named person you're trying to move.

Are there contracts?

Function is a 3-month minimum (long enough to see the loop work), monthly after that. OS is monthly from the start. Packs are scoped one at a time.
