Manifesto · v1.0

We don't do marketing.
We help you become harder to ignore.

Your best buyers are forming opinions before you ever speak to them. They shortlist before sales calls. Investors notice before fundraises. Great hires hear about teams before job specs. Trust got scarce — and most B2B companies are running their organic GTM as random acts of content. We're not.

  1. i. Trust is the new scarce thing. AI made content abundant. The bottleneck moved. The companies that win the next decade build trust on purpose — not by accident.
  2. ii. The market is 20 to 500 people. Not "your TAM". A specific, namable group of buyers, investors, hires, partners and lighthouses. If they don't know you, the rest doesn't matter.
  3. iii. Every action has a purpose. Tied to a named person, a belief you want them to hold, a movement you want to cause. If we can't say what should change, we don't run it.
  4. iv. Measure people, not impressions. We track which named humans moved from cold to advocate this week. Impressions are a side effect, not the goal.
  5. v. Less cold, every week. The only goal that matters. Your market gets less cold. Your story gets sharper. Your company becomes harder to ignore. Compound that for a year.
— Kindling · 2026
